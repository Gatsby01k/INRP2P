# TAKKAR animation direction

## Core rhythm

Every obstacle follows this timing pattern:

1. Threat reveal: 450–800 ms.
2. Mechanical approach: speed pitch rises.
3. Contact anticipation: 90–140 ms.
4. Impact freeze: 65–105 ms depending on device and reduced-motion mode.
5. Compression and debris: 120–220 ms.
6. Survival confirmation: 480–720 ms.
7. Automatic return to motion.

The decision remains available during movement and the survival pause. Nothing else should compete with the Cash Out button.

## Weight rules

- The wheel accelerates with easing; it never jumps to target velocity.
- Wheel rotation is derived from linear speed and visible radius.
- Impact scales the tire wider and shorter before rebound.
- Damage adds wobble gradually; the first impact never creates catastrophic wobble.
- Sparks appear only after enough wear to expose metal.
- Camera shake has a sharp attack and very fast decay.
- Overdrive changes camera distance, light, road energy and pitch together.

## Mobile performance tiers

High:
- DPR up to 1.8;
- 50–75 impact particles;
- full glow and motion ghosts.

Balanced:
- DPR up to 1.35;
- 30–40 impact particles;
- reduced blur.

Battery saver:
- DPR 1.0;
- 15–25 particles;
- simplified tread and speed lines.
